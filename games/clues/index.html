Two-player word game

Guess 10 words with 15 Clues

Ten answers. Fifteen clues. One shared goal.

How to play

Only the explainer looks at the screen. For each answer, say a one-word clue aloud, then tap Use a clue. The guesser keeps guessing; give another one-word clue if needed. Tap Guessed it when they get the answer.

Get all 10 words without going over 15 clues total. Player 1 explains first; switch roles for the next round. No microphone is used.
